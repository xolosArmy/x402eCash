Gate contract

Two-stage rendezvous. One authorization proof. Then stop.

The first tool makes one uncached resource request, preserves the H2B/H3A boundary, and opens a challenge-bound Tonalli handoff only after approval. The second read-only tool reports page-owned ephemeral state.

01

Live contract

The page registers two imperative WebMCP tools and accepts only the exact reviewed 100 XEC HTTP 402 requirement.

02

Human + wallet boundary

Approval creates a 32-byte challenge and opens Tonalli in an isolated auxiliary context. Tonalli H3B remains disabled in production until separately authorized.

03

Verify + stop

The original page binds the callback to the live requirement, rebuilds the exact signed message, verifies the recoverable secp256k1 signature and payer address, then stops without payment.

Project boundary: x402eCash remains the static public laboratory and evidence portal. x402-XEC remains the separate protocol implementation. This experiment does not turn the portal into a payment backend.

Gate H3C prepares an ephemeral Tonalli authorization-proof bridge. H3B is not enabled in production, and this page does not demonstrate an XEC payment, transaction, settlement, broadcast, or resource unlock.
